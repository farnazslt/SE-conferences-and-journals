# Software Engineering Conferences

A curated list of top-tier (**CORE A\* and A**) conferences in software engineering. Deadlines are *typical annual cycles* from recent editions — always check the official call for papers for the exact dates of the edition you are targeting.

| Acronym | Full name | CORE | Focus | Typical deadline | Co-located / related events |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## How to read this table

- **CORE ranking** is the Australian CORE conference ranking (A* = flagship, A = excellent). It is a reputation signal, not a guarantee of fit — read recent proceedings to judge whether your paper belongs there.
- **Typical deadline** is the month papers were due in recent editions. Many venues run *two submission cycles* now (notably ICSE and FSE) — check both.
- **Co-located events** share the venue and week with the main conference, so one trip can cover a workshop, doctoral symposium, or a second conference (the ICSE week is the classic example: ICSE + MSR + ICPC + CHASE + SEAMS).
